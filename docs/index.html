Project Privacy policy

Android attendance app

Take attendance with confidence.

AttendX helps teachers manage classes, rosters, and attendance on their Android phone. Core attendance works offline, with no AttendX account required.

View the project Read the privacy policy

What AttendX does

Fast roll call

Create lecture or lab classes, add students, mark absences, and record a topic for each session.

Portable records

Export attendance to CSV or save a backup file to a location you choose.

Optional Drive backup

When you choose it, save and restore backups in your Google account's private Drive app-data folder.

Your data stays under your control

Attendance is stored on your device. AttendX has no ads or tracking, and the developer does not receive your classes or attendance. Google Drive backup runs only when you choose a Drive action. Read the full privacy policy.
